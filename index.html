Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

"Dream is not that which you see while sleeping, it is something that does not let you sleep."

Explore His Work

Key Scientific Milestones

SLV-III & ISRO

Project Director for India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle, successfully deploying Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980.

IGMDP & Missiles

Spearheaded the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, creating Agni and Prithvi strategic defense missiles.

Pokhran-II Tests

Served as Chief Project Coordinator during the 1998 nuclear tests, establishing India as a nuclear weapons state.

Kalam-Raju Stent

Co-developed a low-cost coronary stent in 1998, making healthcare accessible to millions in rural India.

Contributions & Pillars

Space & Defense

Laid the foundation for India's modern self-reliance in aerospace engineering and strategic ballistic missile technology.

People's President

Transformed Rashtrapati Bhavan into a hub for students, youth, and innovators during his presidency (2002–2007).

Author & Mentor

Authored inspirational bestsellers including Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020.

PURA Initiative

Advocated Provision of Urban Amenities to Rural Areas to bridge rural-urban economic divides across India.

Birthplace & Memorial

Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu

Dr. Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in the island town of Rameswaram. Today, the Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam National Memorial stands here as a tribute to his life, scientific contributions, and noble values.

  • Memorial: Pei Karumbu, Rameswaram
  • House of Kalam: Mosque Street, Rameswaram
